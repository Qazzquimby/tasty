---
title: Crispy chickpeas
time: ~20 min
blurb: Air fryer, paprika, salt. Shake halfway.
---

## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, drained and patted very dry
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Big pinch of salt

## Method

1. Toss everything together in a bowl.
2. Air fry at 200°C for 15–18 minutes, shaking halfway.
3. Let them sit a couple of minutes; they crisp up more as they cool.

## Notes

The drier they go in, the crunchier they come out.
