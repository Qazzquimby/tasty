title: Green smoothie
time: ~5 min
blurb: Banana, spinach, yogurt. Blitz.
---

## Ingredients

- 1 banana (frozen is best)
- Big handful of spinach
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- Splash of milk, a few ice cubes

## Method

Blend until smooth, adding milk until it moves.

## Notes

Add a spoon of peanut butter if it's standing in for a meal.
