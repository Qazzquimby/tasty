---
title: Stovetop popcorn
time: ~8 min
blurb: Three test kernels, then the rest.
---

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup popcorn kernels
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Salt, melted butter if you like

## Method

1. Oil and 3 kernels in a big pot with a lid, medium-high.
2. When all 3 pop, add the rest, take off the heat for 30 seconds, then back on.
3. Shake now and then. Pull it off when the pops are 2–3 seconds apart.
4. Salt straight away.
