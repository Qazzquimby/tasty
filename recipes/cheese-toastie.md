---
title: Cheese toastie
time: ~10 min
blurb: Butter the outside, low heat, lid on.
---

## Ingredients

- 2 slices of bread
- A good handful of grated cheddar
- Butter

## Method

1. Butter one side of each slice.
2. Cheese between the unbuttered sides.
3. Medium-low pan, 3–4 minutes a side, lid on for the first side so the cheese melts before the bread burns.
